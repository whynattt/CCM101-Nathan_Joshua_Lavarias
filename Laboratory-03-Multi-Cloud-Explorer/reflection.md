# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## Mission Reflection

After completing the Multi-Cloud Explorer mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by the wide range of services it provides, especially for computing, storage, databases, networking, and other cloud requirements. I also learned that AWS can support different types of organizations, from startups to large companies.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform have many similarities. All three provide services for virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also provide global infrastructure and scalable cloud resources that allow organizations to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs.

Business requirements have an important role in selecting a cloud platform. An organization needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, type of application, security requirements, scalability, and technical needs. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on AI and Kubernetes may consider Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best solution depends on the specific requirements of the client. I also learned how to compare cloud services and recommend a platform based on business needs.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved after completing this mission. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, created comparison tables, documented client recommendations, and included Linux investigation activities. This helped me organize my work and gave me a portfolio that shows my growing knowledge of cloud computing and practical IT skills.
